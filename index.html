Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

The People's President — Missile Man of India

Biography

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a family of modest means. Despite financial hardships in his childhood, he pursued his passion for aerospace engineering and went on to become one of India's most respected scientists.

He played a pivotal role in India's civilian space program and military missile development, earning him the title "Missile Man of India." His contributions to the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998 further cemented his status as a national hero.

In 2002, Dr. Kalam was elected as the 11th President of India, a role he approached with humility, warmth, and a deep commitment to educating and inspiring the youth of the nation. He was widely known as the "People's President" for his simple lifestyle and accessibility.

Even after his presidency, he continued to teach, write, and mentor students across the country until his sudden passing on 27 July 2015, while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong — a fitting end for a man who dedicated his life to education and inspiration.

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep." — Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Key Achievements

  • 1963 Joined ISRO and contributed to India's first satellite launch vehicle
  • 1998 Led the Pokhran-II nuclear tests as Chief Scientific Advisor
  • 1997 Awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour
  • 2002 Elected as the 11th President of India
  • 2015 Continued inspiring millions through lectures until his final day
